Atelier Workshop

Watch Care & Restoration

Our certified horologists utilize authentic precision tools and genuine parts to service, restore, and maintain your luxury timepieces.

Mechanical Overhaul

Complete disassembly of the movement, ultrasonic ultrasonic bath cleaning, re-lubrication with Swiss horological oils, and multi-position calibration.

Polishing & Lapping

Hand-finishing cases and bracelets preserving factory chamfers, brushed satin grains, and mirror bevels without losing case geometry.

Waterproof & Gaskets

Complete seal replacements and wet/dry vacuum pressure testing to ensure depth-rated water resistance according to ISO standards.

Appraisal & Certification

Laser serial inspection, dial originality assessment, caliber matching, and issuance of formal Certificate of Authenticity for insurance.
